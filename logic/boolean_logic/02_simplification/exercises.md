# Practice: Simplifying and designing circuits

[Course home](../../boolean_logic.md)

**S1.** Simplify xy + xy' + x'z. Draw the original and final networks; count contact occurrences.

**S2.** Simplify:

1. xy + xz
2. x(y + z) + xy'
3. (x + y)(x + y') + x'z
4. xy + xy'z

**S3.** A student replaces xy + z with x(y + z). Show the mistake with an input assignment and explain which conducting path was lost.

**S4.** Build two equivalent networks for xy + xz. Compare all inputs and count contacts. State why the result alone does not prove equal LED current.

**S5.** Design an indicator enabled by x that lights when exactly one of y and z is 1. Supply the truth table, expression and circuit.

**S6.** Three switches x, y and z control a light. Starting at 000 with the light off, changing any one switch must reverse the light. Make a table and derive the expression. You may retain a sum of products.

**S7.** An alarm f is 1 when at least two of x, y and z are 1. Derive the full sum of products from a table, then simplify to xy + xz + yz. Test all inputs.

**S8.** A specification gives f(00) = 0, f(11) = 1, and ? for 01 and 10. Give two different one-contact solutions. Explain why they are acceptable but not equivalent.
